## Tips for Exploring Singapore

### Best Times to Visit

Avoid peak hours (12-2pm, 6-8pm) at popular attractions. Early mornings are ideal for outdoor activities.

### Getting Around

Use the MRT for most locations. Get an EZ-Link card for seamless travel on public transport.

### Save Money

Many attractions offer combo tickets. Hawker centres provide delicious meals under $10.
