### PDPA Compliance

Understanding data protection and privacy requirements

#### Data Collection

We collect personal data only for property management purposes. This includes tenant information, payment records, and communication history.

#### Data Usage

Your data is used to facilitate property rentals, process payments, and provide customer support. We never sell personal information to third parties.

#### Data Retention

Financial records are kept for 7 years as required by law. Other data is retained while you use our services plus 2 years for compliance.

#### Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact us at hellokampungsg@gmail.com for any PDPA-related inquiries.

#### Security Measures

We use bank-level encryption, secure data centers, and regular security audits to protect your information.
